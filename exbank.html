How the load is built

Resistive heat. Inductive demand. Combined on purpose.

A resistive‑only bank proves a generator can make heat. It can't prove the generator holds voltage and frequency when a ship's motors, pumps, and HVAC pull current out of phase. EX‑BANK's R+L composite stages let commissioning engineers dial in a power factor that matches the real vessel or plant load.
